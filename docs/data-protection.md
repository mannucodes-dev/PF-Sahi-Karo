# PF Sahi Karo — Data Protection & Privacy Compliance (DPDP Act)

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Jurisdiction:** India (Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023)

---

## 1. Compliance Architecture

PF Sahi Karo is designed under the principle of **Data Minimization**:
- We collect only the data necessary to provide plain-language rejection guidance.
- We do not store full 12-digit Aadhaar numbers or banking passwords.
- UANs are masked by default (`••••••••7890`).
- Uploaded supporting documents are stored in private encrypted storage and purged after 30 days.

---

## 2. Citizen Rights Implementation

1. **Right to Access Information:** Citizens can view all active and historical claims, transition events, and resubmission records on their authenticated dashboard.
2. **Right to Correction & Completion:** Citizens can update their preferred locale, notification settings, and upload rectified documents.
3. **Right to Erasure:** Citizens can request permanent account and document deletion via the support desk.
4. **Grievance Redressal:** A dedicated grievance channel is provided at `/contact`, with escalation pathways to official Government of India portals (EPFiGMS).
