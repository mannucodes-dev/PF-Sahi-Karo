# PF Sahi Karo — User Research & Citizen Feedback Plan

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Target Demographics:** Salaried workers across Tier 1, 2, and 3 cities in India

---

## 1. Research Objectives

1. Evaluate whether the plain-language decoder significantly reduces citizen anxiety and confusion upon claim rejection.
2. Measure task completion rate for the 3-step resubmission checklist.
3. Validate comprehension of English vs Hindi translations among gig workers and non-tech-savvy users.
4. Assess accessibility for screen reader and keyboard-only users.

---

## 2. Methodology & Cohorts

- **Cohort A (Salaried Corporate Employees):** Evaluate speed of understanding rejection remarks and KYC requirements.
- **Cohort B (Industrial & Gig Workers):** Test low-bandwidth mobile viewports, Hindi language clarity, and assisted digital pathways.
- **Cohort C (Accessibility Users):** Test with NVDA on Windows and VoiceOver on iOS/macOS.
