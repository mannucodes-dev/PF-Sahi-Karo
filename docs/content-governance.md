**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Responsible Board:** PF Sahi Karo Compliance Review Board

---

## 1. Content Verification Standards

Every rejection reason explanation, action checklist, and timeline published on PF Sahi Karo must satisfy the following governance rules:
1. **Direct Policy Grounding:** Every rule must be mapped to an official published circular, Standard Operating Procedure (SOP), or statutory guideline issued by the EPFO or Ministry of Labour & Employment.
2. **Plain-Language Translation:** Cryptic technical remarks must be translated into simple, easy-to-understand language in both English and Hindi.
3. **Clear Division of Responsibility:** Instructions must distinguish between actions the citizen can perform independently and actions requiring employer Digital Signature Certificate (DSC) or EPFO field office action.
4. **Mandatory Metadata:** Each record must state the `source_url`, `source_reference`, `reviewed_at` timestamp, and `reviewed_by` authority.
5. **No Machine Translation of High-Impact Advice:** Legal and compliance guidance is manually translated and verified by human compliance specialists.
